# Return and Refund Policy

## Overview

We want you to be completely satisfied with our service. If you're not happy, we offer refunds under the following conditions.

## Monthly Subscriptions

- **Full Refund Period**: 14 days from charge date
- **Eligibility**: All monthly plans (Free upgrade to Premium, Premium, Enterprise Monthly)
- **Process**: Automatic upon request within the refund window
- **Timeline**: Refunds processed within 3-5 business days

## Annual Subscriptions

- **Prorated Refund Period**: First 30 days from purchase
- **Calculation**: Refund = Total paid - (Monthly rate × Months used)
- **After 30 Days**: No refunds available for annual plans
- **Exception**: Service outages exceeding 48 hours may qualify for credits

## Duplicate Charges

- **Policy**: Always refunded in full, no questions asked
- **Detection**: Our system automatically flags potential duplicates
- **Process**: Contact support with order IDs for immediate processing
- **Timeline**: Expedited processing within 1-2 business days

## Non-Refundable Items

- Add-on purchases after 7 days
- Custom integration development fees
- Training session fees (unless cancelled 48+ hours in advance)
- Domain registration fees

## How to Request a Refund

1. Email support@example.com with your order ID
2. Or use the in-app "Request Refund" button
3. Or contact our chat support

## Special Circumstances

For refunds outside these policies, please contact our support team. We review each case individually and may grant exceptions for:
- Extended illness or hardship
- Documented technical issues preventing use
- Significant service degradation
